WORLD IN A JAR — DATA & DELETION

Last updated: 20 October 2026

REMOVE LOCAL GAME DATA

Open Android Settings > Apps > World in a Jar > Storage > Clear storage / Clear data. Names vary by device. This removes the app's local progress, settings, logs and local recovery files. It is permanent. Separately exported copies must be removed separately. This screen does not delete anything automatically.

STARTING OVER IS DIFFERENT

Starting a new game resets gameplay, but may retain settings, identity metadata or diagnostic/recovery files. It is not a full privacy deletion. This release has no active cloud or account service; signing out or deleting a cloud placeholder is not an account-erasure service.
