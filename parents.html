WORLD IN A JAR — FOR PARENTS AND CHILDREN

Last updated: 22 October 2026

FOR CHILDREN

You can play without telling us your name, email, age or address. Your garden and levels stay on your device. There is no chat with strangers, advertising or real-money buying in this version. Ask a trusted adult if you need help. Do not send us your photo, school, address, password or other private information.

FOR PARENTS AND GUARDIANS

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over, including teenagers and adults; it is not directed at children under 13. You can supervise play and use your device's family and screen-time controls. The app does not create a child account or ask for personal details to unlock play. No online behavioural advertising or remote gameplay analytics is active.

Please handle support requests for a child and avoid including their identifying information. Contact kristicodetaylor@gmail.com if a child has accidentally sent personal information so we can review and remove it where appropriate. See the Privacy Policy and Data & Deletion notice for details. Reading a notice does not authorise a future purchase or consent to future collection.

PLAY COMFORTABLY

Take breaks when needed. Sound, vibration and Reduced Motion can be changed in Settings. A booster example uses a separate practice board; real booster use spends stock. Spending earned game currency does not charge a payment card in this version. Clearing app storage permanently removes the local game.
