WORLD IN A JAR — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 22 October 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

This version plays offline. Your game stays on your device. There are no active advertising, account, cloud-save, purchase, remote analytics or crash-upload services. We do not sell your information or use it for targeted advertising. A parent can help a child read this policy and contact us.

WHAT THE GAME STORES

The app stores your levels, discoveries, Jar buildings and positions, inventory, virtual currencies, earned boosters, permanent booster choices, rewards and transaction history. It also stores presentation settings and random local profile/save identifiers, which are not hardware identifiers.

Limited event history and error information help the app diagnose problems locally. The profile keeps the latest 100 event entries. The separate error file is rotated after reaching approximately 128 KiB. These records are not automatically sent to us.

Save files can include recovery backups, temporary files and retained copies of damaged saves. These remain in the app's local storage. The current Android release configuration disables automatic app-data backup; this is not an online backup service. Copies you or your device tools export separately are outside the game's control.

WHAT THIS VERSION DOES NOT DO

The game does not request your name, email, birth date, contacts, location, camera or microphone to play. It does not use an advertising identifier or send your gameplay to an advertising or analytics service. There is no chat, public profile, user-uploaded content or player-to-player messaging. Optional vibration is controlled in Settings.

There are no active app accounts or app-operated servers. Adapter code for possible future services does not activate those services. Any future change to data handling will require updated information and any legally necessary safeguards before activation.

WHY DATA IS USED

Local data makes the game work, resumes your progress, remembers your choices, prevents duplicate rewards and supports recovery. It is required for those features and is processed on your device. Where personal-data law applies, the basis is providing the requested game functionality and our legitimate interests in reliable, secure operation, as applicable. We do not rely on an advertising-consent checkbox to operate this offline version.

IF YOU CONTACT SUPPORT

Email support is optional and separate from ordinary gameplay. We receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Please send only what is needed; do not send passwords, identity documents or unrelated personal information. Parents or guardians should contact us on a child's behalf and avoid including the child's identifying details.

We use messages to answer requests, resolve faults and handle privacy or legal matters. The applicable basis is responding to your request or providing support, our legitimate interest in resolving problems, or a legal obligation when one applies. Our email provider, Google, processes the correspondence under the applicable service and privacy terms. Email delivery may involve processing outside your country; legally required transfer protections must apply.

Support messages are normally removed from the active support mailbox within 12 months after closure, sooner when no longer needed or when an applicable deletion request requires it. Accidental identifying information about a child is removed promptly when not needed for a permitted response. A specific legal duty or dispute may require limited records for longer; we will explain an applicable exception. Mail-provider backup retention is governed by that provider's service arrangements.

SHARING AND SECURITY

The app does not automatically transmit its local profile or diagnostics to us or third-party services. If you voluntarily email information, it reaches the support mailbox and its email provider. Information may also be disclosed where a binding legal obligation requires it. We do not sell, rent or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Local files use Android's app storage protections. We minimise support information and restrict its use to resolving the request. No device, storage system or email service can promise perfect security. Keep your device protected and avoid sending unnecessary attachments.

CHILDREN AND PARENTS

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. It does not ask players to create an account, disclose contact details, upload content or enable tracking. It has no active ads or real-money purchase flow. Parents can supervise play, review this policy, manage device settings and remove local game data.

If you believe a child under 13 has sent personal information to support, contact the address above so we can review and remove it where appropriate. We do not require a child to provide personal information to keep playing. Before any feature that collects children's information online is introduced, the relevant parental notice, consent, minimisation and other legal protections must be in place. Reading this notice is not parental consent to a future feature.

RETENTION AND DELETION ON YOUR DEVICE

Local game data remains until it is replaced in normal play or you remove it. Starting over resets gameplay; it is not a complete purge of every setting, log or recovery file.

To remove the app's local data, use Android Settings > Apps > World in a Jar > Storage > Clear storage / Clear data. Labels vary by device. This permanently removes progress and the app's local recovery copies. Separately exported copies must be removed separately. We cannot remotely find or delete an offline profile that has never been sent to us.
